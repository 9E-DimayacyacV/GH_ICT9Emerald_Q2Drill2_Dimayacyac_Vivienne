ABOUT US

Since 1966, our school has been dedicated to championing the rights of the child by providing an exceptional Montessori education in the Philippines. We are a Montessori school that makes use of material for visual learning to help students understand the lessons on a deeper basis. Our school has always prioritized independence but as well as knowing how to work with others.
